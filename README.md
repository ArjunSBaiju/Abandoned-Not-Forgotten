# Abandoned, Not Forgotten
**Off-World Archaeology, Orbital Proof & First-Person Hardware Chronicles**

Built for the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge**, *Abandoned, Not Forgotten* is an interactive planetary exploration experience that brings dormant Moon and Mars hardware back to life through real NASA surface maps, orbital proof, and first-person storytelling[cite: 1].

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://nimble-raindrop-e2515a.netlify.app](https://nimble-raindrop-e2515a.netlify.app/)

---

## Overview

Humanity has left behind dozens of rovers, landers, and ascent stages across the Solar System—silent monuments to our earliest steps off Earth. *Abandoned, Not Forgotten* transforms these dormant artifacts from static coordinates into living narratives, letting users explore where they rest today and hear their stories in their own words[cite: 1].

---

## Key Features

- **Interactive Planetary Maps (Moon & Mars):** Explore real NASA planetary projections (LRO / MRO) featuring clickable beacons across 8 iconic off-world artifacts—4 on the Moon (*Surveyor 3*, *Apollo 11 Eagle*, *Apollo 15 Rover*, *Apollo 17 Challenger*) and 4 on Mars[cite: 1].
- **Chronological Jump:** Navigate spaceflight history across key milestones from **1967** (*Surveyor 3*)
